PROJECT 01 — URBAN ANALYSIS & HIGH-RISE

MANHATTAN SKYSCRAPER

Location: Manhattan, NY • Concept Phase
BROADWAY COMPOSITIONAL AXIS DIAGRAM URBAN GRID ANALYSIS

Figure 1.1 — Manhattan street grid context & Broadway diagonal alignment study.

Design Concept Statement

"Since our site is located on the same strip as Broadway, we decided to use this diagonal line as the main compositional axis of the project."

The urban layout of Manhattan's grid is interrupted by Broadway's historical path. The skyscraper volume articulates set-backs and angled facade cuts aligned with this diagonal axis, allowing natural sunlight penetration and visual connectivity with surrounding avenues.

Structural & Spatial Design Criteria

• Bearing Wall System

"Designed using load-bearing walls aligned consistently between upper and lower floors to guarantee structural continuity and even weight distribution."

• Solid Reinforced Slab

"Complemented with 20 cm thick solid reinforced concrete slabs across floor levels."

• Parapet & Drainage

"A 40 cm high perimeter parapet wall was designed to ensure proper rainwater runoff, along with 20 cm levelling fill for roof insulation."

• Grid Modulation

"Strict structural modulation aligning beam lines and bearing walls to distribute loads efficiently and maintain structural simplicity."
